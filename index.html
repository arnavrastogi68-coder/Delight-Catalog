OUR HERITAGE

A Legacy of Moradabad Craftsmanship

Built on a family tradition of fine metal craftsmanship that continues through House of Delight.

Delight Metal Works (House of Delight) is an integrated designing and manufacturing firm started in the year 1970 by Mr. Jagdish Saran Rastogi in Moradabad—India’s celebrated "Brass City". What began as a dedicated family workshop has flourished over five decades into a premier manufacturing and trading house spanning Pure Brass, Pure Copper, Authentic Kansa (Bronze), and Stainless Steel metalware, serving an extensive dealer and export network across India and globally. Because every piece is individually turned and polished by traditional Moradabad artisans, subtle variations in weight, polish, and texture are hallmarks of authentic metalcraft.
