Heritage of Moradabad Craftsmanship
by Jagdish Saran Rastogi

Delight Metal Works is an integrated designing and manufacturing firm started in the year 1970 by Mr. Jagdish Saran Rastogi in Moradabad—India’s celebrated "Brass City". What began as a family workshop is today a premier manufacturing and trading house spanning Pure Brass, Pure Copper, Authentic Kansa (Bronze), and Stainless Steel, serving an extensive dealer network across India and globally.
